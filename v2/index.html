Scale. Each sentence is scored −10 (hawkish) to +10 (dovish) by three models independently; the published figure is the sentence-level mean across models. The scale is absolute, not directional — holding at the lower bound reads dovish, holding at a restrictive level reads hawkish — so a flat line means an unchanged level.
Conviction is the spread between the three models on the latest statement: tight means they agree, wide means the text is genuinely ambiguous.
